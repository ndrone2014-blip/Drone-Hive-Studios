DRONE HIVE STUDIOS

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 29, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Drone Hive Studios (“we,” “us,” or “our”) handles information when you use Community AutoMod, our Discord bot. We aim to collect only information needed to operate, secure, and support the bot.

1. Information We May Process

  • Discord identifiers: server (guild) IDs, user IDs, channel IDs, and role IDs needed to identify servers, members, settings, and bot actions.
  • Moderation records: warning records and related moderation information, such as the affected user, moderator, reason, and action details, when those features are used.
  • Server configuration: settings for automated moderation, logging, autoroles, welcome or goodbye messages, and other enabled features.
  • Event and log information: information about relevant server events, such as message edits or deletions and member or role changes, when the corresponding logging features are enabled and the bot has the required Discord permissions.
  • Message content: messages may be processed to provide automated moderation and related enabled features. Depending on server configuration, limited message details may appear in moderation logs. We do not intend to use message content for advertising.

2. How We Use Information

We use information to provide bot features, apply server settings, maintain moderation records, create configured logs, troubleshoot errors, protect the bot from misuse, and respond to support requests.

3. Storage and Retention

Bot data may be stored in a database used by the bot, including a local SQLite database when the bot is self-hosted. We retain information for as long as it is needed to provide the relevant feature, maintain requested records, or meet legitimate operational and security needs. Exact retention periods may depend on the server operator’s configuration and how the bot is hosted. We do not promise that every record is automatically deleted after a fixed period.

4. Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information may be processed by Discord as part of the platform’s operation, and may be disclosed if required by law or reasonably necessary to protect users, the service, or our rights. Server administrators may be able to view information surfaced by bot commands or configured server logs.

5. Your Choices and Requests

Server administrators can remove the bot and disable its features. If you have a question or request about information associated with your Discord user ID, contact us through the support server below and include enough detail for us to understand the request. We may need to verify the request and coordinate with the server owner or administrator. Deleting a server’s local database or backups may require action by the person who hosts the bot.

6. Security

We use reasonable measures appropriate to the way the bot is operated to protect stored information. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Children

The bot is intended for use in Discord communities. Server owners and administrators are responsible for ensuring that their server and use of the bot comply with applicable age requirements, Discord policies, and local laws.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the bot changes. We will post the revised version here and update the effective date.

9. Contact

For privacy questions or data requests, contact the Drone Hive Studios team through the Community AutoMod support server: https://discord.gg/YyrenepW3C.
